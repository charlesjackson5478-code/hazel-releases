# Hazel

**Private, serverless chat that lives on your phone.**

## Download the latest version

**Hazel 6.6** — [Hazel-6.6.apk](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/charlesjackson5478-code/hazel-releases/main/v6.6/Hazel-6.6.apk)

Open the link on your Android phone, then install. Hazel also tells you inside the app when a newer version is ready.

## What's new in 6.6

- 📵 **Calls end with their chat** — erase or leave a chat during a call and the call ends on both phones
- 🎮 **Full-screen games keep every square the same size**
- 🔔 **Always online in one tap** if a phone holds back wake-ups

## In 6.5

- 🔔 **Messages arrive while Hazel is closed** — fetched the instant the phone is woken, and kept safe
- 📞 **Calls ring a sleeping phone** — full-screen call with Answer and Decline
- ✅ **Delivery check** — Settings → Notifications shows anything holding Hazel back
- 🌍 **Earth card** — tap a dot, Ask to chat right there
- 🎮 **Full-screen games**

## In 6.2

- 🗑️ **Leave and delete are instant** — the chat goes at once and nothing else waits behind it
- 🌍 **Earth re-asks** — remove a chat and you can ask the same person again straight away
- 🔔 **Hazel-code requests wake the other phone** — even when their Hazel is closed
- 💬 **Chats open at the newest message** and stay steady while you type
- 📞 **Compact call cards**, bordered join notices, rounded video tile

## In 6.1

- ⚡ **Finds people faster** — chat requests, invite codes and Earth connect in seconds
- 📞 **Call cards on their own sides** — time at the bottom, tap one to call back
- 📳 **Tab vibration is off** unless you switch it on in Settings

## Before that, in 6.0

- 📞 **Call cards in chat** — missed, declined and finished calls appear as colour cards
- 🎛️ **Tuck-away call controls** — one slim bar, swipe down to hide, swipe up to bring it back
- 🔔 **Their own sounds** — a soft beep while connecting, a double ring when their phone rings
- 🪟 **Floating video window** — keep the call visible while you use other apps
- 🕒 **Exact last seen** — today's messages always show today
- 🔗 **Invite links open smoothly**
- 🛡️ **Stronger privacy checks**

## Safe by design

Every update is checked against a SHA-256 fingerprint before it installs, so only the real Hazel can update Hazel.
